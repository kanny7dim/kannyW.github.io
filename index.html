Kanny Wu

Drawing from a background as a classical pianist, Kanny Wu works through sound, image, and sculpture as media to explore perception, fragility, and the position of materials, bodies, and memories in time. Each work creates a dialogue between sensory experience and the world, emphasizing vulnerability, identity, and coexistence.
