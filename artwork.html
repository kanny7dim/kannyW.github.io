Deep Listening

Deep listening unfolds as a sensorial practice — a way of attuning to the fragile boundaries between body, matter, and the world. It engages the auditory perception, opening the awareness to the smallest vibrations and resonances. Through attentive listening, the audience can experience sound as a living presence that interacts with space, time, and memory, enhancing the perception of fragility, transience, and intimacy. These auditory explorations are deeply intertwined with materials, bodily gestures, and environmental context, inviting reflection on impermanence, sensitivity, and the hidden connections that shape our lived experience.

Audio Tracks

These tracks can be played individually or simultaneously, allowing the audience to freely combine sounds.

By means of sound, photography, and sculpture, they probe the unknown,
perceiving fragility, grace, and resilience; all is inscribed, all is traceable.
