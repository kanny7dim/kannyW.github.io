Deep Listening

Deep listening unfolds as a sensorial practice — a way of attuning to the fragile boundaries between body, matter, and the world. It involves active perception of environmental sounds, internal bodily resonance, and the spatial qualities of material objects. Through these experiences, one cultivates a heightened sensitivity to subtle vibrations, rhythms, and textures, embracing the ephemeral and transient nature of sound and presence.

Audio Tracks

These tracks can be played individually or simultaneously, allowing the audience to freely combine sounds.

By means of sound, photography, and sculpture, they probe the unknown,
perceiving fragility, grace, and resilience; all is inscribed, all is traceable.
