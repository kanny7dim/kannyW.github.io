Deep Listening

Deep listening unfolds as a sensorial practice, attuning to the fragile boundaries between body, matter, and the world. It emphasizes the perception of subtle sounds and textures, exploring how listening can create a dialogue with space, memory, and sensation. Each track reveals hidden layers, allowing personal interpretation and interaction.

Audio Tracks

These tracks can be played individually or simultaneously, allowing the audience to freely combine sounds and perceive overlapping textures.
