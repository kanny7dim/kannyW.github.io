Deep Listening

Deep listening unfolds as a sensorial practice — a way of attuning to the fragile boundaries between body, matter, and the world. It encourages a heightened awareness of sound, space, and time, where one can perceive subtle vibrations, echoes, and the resonance of materials. This practice extends beyond mere hearing, fostering an intimate connection with the ephemeral qualities of our environment. The audio tracks below can be played individually or simultaneously, allowing for dynamic interactions and personal experiences of soundscapes.
