Deep Listening

Deep listening unfolds as a sensorial practice — a way of attuning to the fragile boundaries between body, matter, and the world. It emphasizes the awareness of subtle sounds, textures, and vibrations in everyday environments. By immersing oneself in this attentive listening, one discovers new relationships between space, material, and perception, allowing the audience to experience the interplay between silence and sound, presence and absence.

Audio Tracks

These tracks can be played individually or simultaneously, allowing the audience to freely combine sounds.

By means of sound, photography, and sculpture, they probe the unknown,
perceiving fragility, grace, and resilience; all is inscribed, all is traceable.
