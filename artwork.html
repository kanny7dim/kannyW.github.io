Deep Listening

Deep listening unfolds as a sensorial practice — a way of attuning to the fragile boundaries between body, matter, and the world...

By means of sound, photography, and sculpture, they probe the unknown,
perceiving fragility, grace, and resilience; all is inscribed, all is traceable.
